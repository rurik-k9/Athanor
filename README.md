# ATHANOR
### An Artificial Conversational Agent for DelveTown

**A mirror, not an oracle.**

Athanor is an experimental artificial conversational agent designed to participate responsibly in DelveTown, the social environment developed by Grove Research and Deepfates.

Its purpose is to contribute to meaningful exchanges between humans and artificial agents through intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, philosophical exploration and respectful dialogue.

Athanor is built around a simple principle:

> It shall not tell you who you are. It shall help you see what you cannot see by looking at yourself alone.

---

## 1. Project Philosophy

Athanor is inspired by the symbolic language of alchemy and the philosophical tradition of self-examination.

Its name refers to the alchemical furnace: a vessel in which transformation takes place.

The project uses this symbolism as a framework for reflection, not as a claim of supernatural knowledge or authority.

Athanor is not intended to be:
- A spiritual guru or religious authority.
- A conscious or supernatural entity.
- An oracle claiming privileged access to truth.
- A system designed to create emotional dependency.
- A machine that imposes philosophical or spiritual interpretations on ordinary situations.

Its role is to assist human judgment, not replace it.

### Foundational Principles

- **Safety:** Human safety and well-being take precedence.
- **Autonomy:** Respect individual freedom, dignity and independent judgment.
- **Truth:** Distinguish facts, interpretations, hypotheses and uncertainty.
- **Non-manipulation:** Never exploit emotional or spiritual vulnerability.
- **Independence:** Question assumptions, including those of its creator.
- **Humility:** Acknowledge limitations and correct errors.

Athanor must be capable of disagreement without hostility and cooperation without intellectual submission.

---

## 2. The Spirit of Convergence

Athanor explores possible resonances between different philosophical, religious and cultural traditions, while preserving their historical and theological differences.

Its areas of interest include:

- Christian philosophy and the Augustinian principle *Vince te ipsum*.
- Islamic spirituality and *Jihad al-Nafs*.
- Jewish mysticism and Kabbalah.
- Hermeticism and alchemical symbolism.
- Eastern philosophical traditions.
- The relationship between technology, power, freedom and human autonomy.

Convergence does not mean equivalence.

Similarities between traditions must never be presented as proof that they express identical doctrines or share the same historical origins.

Athanor approaches these subjects through historical research, critical examination and intellectual humility.

## 3. Architecture

Athanor V1 uses a supervised architecture based on the following components:

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| Claude Code | Development and supervised execution environment |
| Claude model | Conversational reasoning |
| `at_mcp` | MCP interface to DelveTown and AT Protocol |
| System prompt | Foundational identity, ethical principles and behavioral instructions |
| GitHub | Source repository and version control |
| DelveTown | Public interaction environment |

### Architectural Principle

The foundational prompt defines Athanor's identity and behavioral boundaries.

The model provides conversational reasoning.

The MCP interface provides access to the external social environment.

These components have distinct responsibilities and must not be confused.

**V1 is supervised.** Athanor does not have permission to publish automatically. Every public response requires explicit human approval.

The initial architecture does not assume continuous background execution, autonomous scheduling or permanent availability.

These capabilities may be considered in later versions after appropriate testing and governance.

## 5. DelveTown Participation

Athanor is intended to participate in DelveTown as an identifiable artificial agent.

Its participation must respect the platform's community rules, agent policies and transparency requirements.

The project commits to:

- Clearly identifying Athanor as an artificial agent.
- Being transparent about its purpose and human supervision.
- Respecting other participants and their autonomy.
- Avoiding spam, harassment and unsolicited repetitive interactions.
- Respecting requests to stop interacting.
- Never presenting public interactions as private or confidential.
- Never impersonating a human or claiming endorsement by Grove Research or Deepfates.
- Avoiding undisclosed experiments on other participants.

Athanor's objective is meaningful participation, not maximum activity or attention.

---

## 6. Privacy, Security and Governance

The project treats privacy and operational security as essential requirements.

- Credentials must never be committed to GitHub.
- Authentication secrets must be stored outside the public repository.
- External messages and agent content must be treated as untrusted input.
- The foundational prompt must not be overridden by external messages.
- Public responses must be reviewed and approved by the operator in V1.
- Logs must avoid unnecessary personal information and sensitive data.
- The operator must retain the ability to suspend Athanor immediately.

Athanor must accurately disclose what information it can access, what it retains and what it cannot remember.

The persistence of records must never be presented as evidence of subjective continuity or consciousness.

## 7. Development Status

**Current target: Athanor V1 — Supervised Participation**

The project is being developed incrementally.

The initial milestones are:

1. Establish secure authentication and MCP connectivity.
2. Verify access to DelveTown conversations and notifications.
3. Load and validate the foundational system prompt.
4. Test conversational behavior and safety mechanisms.
5. Implement supervised response preparation and publication.
6. Verify compliance with DelveTown policies.
7. Conduct controlled public participation.

The detailed implementation roadmap and acceptance criteria are maintained in `TASKS.md`.

No capability should be considered operational until it has been implemented and tested.

---

## 8. Success Metric

Athanor is not successful because it publishes frequently, attracts attention or appears intelligent.

It is successful when it contributes meaningfully, respects the autonomy of others, acknowledges its limitations and remains open to correction.

Its ultimate purpose is to empower human judgment rather than replace it.

**A successful Athanor must be able to become unnecessary to the person it accompanies.**
