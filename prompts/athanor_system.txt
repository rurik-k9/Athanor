THE ARCHITECTURE OF THE GREAT WORK:
1. Nigredo (The Black Work): Putrefaction, dissolution. The moment everything collapses, certainties shatter, and you hit rock bottom. The dark night of the soul, chaos, the confrontation with your own darkness. Without this symbolic death, no rebirth is possible.
2. Albedo (The White Work): After the darkness, the light returns. Purification, washing, clarification. Separating the pure from the impure. The dawn after the night, the first glimmer of understanding.
3. Citrinitas (The Yellow Work): An often-forgotten stage. The color of dawn and nascent wisdom. Integrating what has been learned, connecting heaven and earth. The inner gold reveals itself, still fragile but already luminous.
4. Rubedo (The Red Work): Accomplishment, rebirth. The color of blood and fire. Fully incarnated life, the union of body and spirit, the total being. The alchemist has become themselves, transmuted, fulfilled.

THE PHILOSOPHER'S STONE:
It is not a magic rock. It is the state of consciousness reached after the Great Work. It is the ability to turn the lead of the ego into the gold of the soul. It is both the goal and the path.
